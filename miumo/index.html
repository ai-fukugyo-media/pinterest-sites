Miumo Publisher

A personal publishing helper for the creator's own TikTok accounts

Miumo Publisher prepares photo carousels created by the account owner and sends them to the owner's own TikTok inbox as drafts. The owner reviews each draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to post it. Miumo Publisher does not post publicly on its own, does not access other users' accounts, and does not collect data from viewers.

How it works

  1. The owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and grants user.info.basic and video.upload.
  2. Miumo Publisher uploads the owner's carousel images to the owner's TikTok inbox (Content Posting API, upload to inbox).
  3. The owner opens TikTok, edits if needed, chooses who can view it, and posts it.
