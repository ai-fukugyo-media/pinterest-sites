Privacy Policy

Miumo Publisher · Effective 2026-10-02

What we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile information of the authorizing account (user.info.basic: open ID, display name, avatar), used only to confirm which of the owner's accounts is connected.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, used only to upload the owner's drafts (video.upload).

How it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never published, sold or shared. No data about viewers or other TikTok users is collected.

Retention and deletion

Revoking the app in TikTok invalidates the tokens; local copies are deleted when access is revoked or on request.
